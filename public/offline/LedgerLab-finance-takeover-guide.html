01 / YOUR FIRST DAY

Establish control before preparing a report

Read the handover and managing director's monthly brief. Establish cash, supplier continuity, payroll deadlines, overdue receivables and the starting trial balance. Create a first-week action plan with owners and evidence.

A messy February–December start includes three predecessor errors. Investigate the additional insurance release, duplicated accrual and customer receipt moved into suspense. Correct their effects without deleting the inherited entries or recording a second bank receipt. January starts from the agreed opening trial balance instead.

02 / PROCESS THE EVIDENCE

The source queue is your unfinished desk

Invoices and credits

Open the source. Use Capture invoice and select the correct ledger coding. The invoice's original reference and issue date remain intact. Capture does not create a second copy of the source.

An invoice may also have a settlement in the same month. Recording the invoice alone does not mean all work associated with that source is complete.

Receipts, payments and journals

Record cash separately from invoices. Use the correct customer or supplier, source reference and transaction date. Prepare recurring and adjusting journals from the supporting facts.

Wrong entries affect the live books. Reverse and replace an error; do not overwrite the audit trail. Extra free-practice entries are outside the fixed scenario and can block its close.

04 / PREPARE THE CLOSE

Own the adjustments and the explanation

Work through accruals, prepayments, revenue cut-off, deferred income, stock, fixed assets and relevant tax controls. Save supporting balance-sheet reconciliations in the existing workbench.

Read the monthly and year-to-date financial statements. Explain budget differences, cash versus profit, inherited corrections, recurring performance and non-cash adjustments. Submit the role deliverables with your calculated figures, evidence and recommended actions.

Save drafts before changing tasks or views. Saved role and forecast drafts persist; unsaved form changes can be lost during navigation. The global notebook has separate navigation-preserving draft handling.

05 / FINANCIAL-MANAGER TRACK

Convert the ledger into forward decisions

13-week cash forecast

Start from the live month-end operating bank. Enter collections, supplier payments, payroll, tax, overheads, capital spending and funding. Keep payments positive and avoid double-counting overheads already included in supplier payments.

Review reserve headroom and the stress case: 85% of receipts collected two weeks later, with no automatic funding. Receipts moving beyond the window are not written off.

Commercial recommendation

Document collection assumptions, payment timing, liquidity actions and approval needs. Evaluate the supplied capital proposal with its stated cash flows and hurdle rate.

A proposal is not an incurred purchase. Recommend approval, deferral or rejection with reasons; do not post the investment merely because it appears in a management brief.

06 / CLOSE, RETAIN, PROGRESS

Finish one month before opening the next

Review the source-posting, double-entry, bank, deliverable and forecast gates. Record your close conclusion. Once closed, either review that month, reopen the latest close with a reason, or release the next month.

Financial changes reopen submitted current-month reporting and forecast work so stale conclusions do not retain a completed status. Export the full backup and Work portfolio at milestones.

December adds year-end estimates, tax and AFS work. The missing comparative, subsequent-event, going-concern and approval evidence must remain visible. No complete 2026 operating year is generated.

KNOW WHAT THE APP DOES NOT PROVE

A practice close is not professional sign-off

Source-linked figures, accounting effects, arithmetic, evidence references and required fields are checked. Written judgement and forecast realism are not independently assessed. Text length is only a completion threshold. Use your portfolio for review with an experienced industry accountant or financial manager.

The existing fictional case is AUD and calendar 2025, with disclosed teaching tax/payroll assumptions. It is not a South African tax engine, a live banking or filing system, a multi-entity consolidation system, or a complete statutory AFS package.

The same business prompts currently recur across months. This release introduces ownership and progression; it is not yet a fully branching simulation of every finance-management responsibility.
