# LedgerLab Workstation v3: Day-by-Day Finance Job

7 September 2026. Downloadable local workstation and complete source continuation. The hosted application has not been changed.

## Start here

Open `LedgerLab-Workstation-v3-Day-by-Day.html` in a current desktop browser. There is no installation or external runtime script download. This is an operational local accounting workstation, not a screenshot or static design.

A fresh case starts on **1 July 2025**, in the **financial-manager** role with a **messy handover**. The first open window is **Today**. Click **Read onboarding email**, read the CFO's instructions, and click **Open assignment**. The predecessor's historical ledger is already carried forward; the active month is not done for you.

To choose another joining month, role or handover, use **Today → Backup / case settings → Start a different takeover**. January through December 2025, financial accountant/financial manager and supported/messy handovers remain available. Export existing work before replacing a case. January has no earlier 2025 accountant activity in which to plant takeover corrections.

### Bring your existing v2/v2.1 work across

Save your journals, responses, notebook and workbooks in the old HTML, then use **Case settings → Export/download backup**. Open v3 and restore that backup through **Case settings**. A previous monthly case remains monthly until you choose **Enable day-by-day mode for this case**. Enabling preserves its saved work and moves the simulated date far enough forward to keep already-used evidence available. It does not silently turn a worked month back into a fresh first day.

For the clean onboarding experience, start a new daily takeover after exporting the old case. Keep backups from both. Do not assume different HTML filenames or browser profiles share storage.

## The daily routine

**1. Read new correspondence.** The CFO's onboarding explains the business, inherited records, responsibilities, date rules and daily routine. The departing accountant identifies handover records and unresolved points. Management requests include the required output, internal due date, instructions and links to evidence.

**2. Work from Today.** The main screen highlights one next action. Its focus queue and filters distinguish due today, overdue, newly arrived, completed and other open work. It does not dump the entire future month onto the first day's task list. The default July case opens with six available task instances and five emails.

**3. Use the correct tool.** Open the assignment, inspect the source, process an invoice or journal in Accounting, match the bank entry separately, and use a workbook for supporting calculations. Formal-deliverable links open the particular deliverable. The forecast assignment opens the actual editable forecast. Saving a spreadsheet does not post a journal or submit a reconciliation.

**4. Save and submit the requested output.** Management responses have evidence references, figures where appropriate, a control decision where relevant and written analysis. You may attach an already-saved working paper. Use Save draft while investigating and Submit response when the output is ready. Incorrect submitted figures can be saved for diagnostic feedback, but do not pass the task.

**5. Check and end the day.** Use **Check today's work**, review missing/incorrect items, save open forms and workbooks, then click **End day** or the taskbar PC date. Choose the next calendar day, next working day or another permitted date. Record an end-of-day note and acknowledge outstanding or skipped deadlines when required.

A task status of Complete means its defined numerical/control/completion checks pass. It does not endorse the quality of the written analysis or constitute independent management approval.

## The PC date is not the accounting period

The date shown on the desktop/taskbar is a fictional scenario date. Changing it does not change your real computer clock. It advances only when you explicitly choose a date; there are no real-time timers or background email deliveries.

Document dates and delivery dates are intentionally different. The accounting date remains the source/effective date, not the day the email arrived. An invoice dated 3 July can arrive on 4 July and still be posted with its appropriate 3 July accounting date. Future actual posting, unavailable source capture and premature full-month answer review are rejected by the command layer, not merely hidden buttons.

| Evidence | Daily-mode availability rule |
|---|---|
| Inherited records from before your appointment | Already in the archive. |
| Sales invoices and ordinary dated source records | Their issue/effective date, subject to the active accounting period. |
| Supplier invoices and supplier credit notes | One working day after their source date. |
| Bank movements | Next working day. Before month-end, the bank file is an explicitly partial as-of extract with only delivered movements. |
| Payroll register, payslips and HR review pack | On the 23rd, or the preceding working day when the 23rd is a weekend. The scheduled pay date is still separate. |
| Monthly bank, customer and supplier statements; monthly fixed-asset register | First working day after month-end. |
| Budgets, policies and other genuinely forward-looking plans | May be known before the periods they concern. They are not future actual transactions. |

The internal calendar treats Monday–Friday as working days. It does **not** model public holidays, statutory filing extensions, local payroll cut-offs or intraday deadlines. Stated deadlines are end of day and are fictional company targets.

## A real close takes place after month-end

For July, closing statements arrive on **1 August 2025**. The PC may be in August while **July remains the open accounting month**. You continue to post supported July adjustments, complete July reconciliations and submit July reporting.

The close window runs through the tenth working day after month-end. Check, submit month for review, close the accounting month and release the next month remain separate actions. Moving the PC date does not do any of them automatically.

This version has **one open accounting period**, not simultaneous posting into two periods. After July closes and you release August, the PC date does not rewind; August records that have already arrived are available as a backlog. Original deadlines remain visible, so this limitation can produce a next-month backlog while you finish the prior close. It is not a fully parallel real-world close calendar. There is no generated 2026 operating year, although the December close can take place in January 2026.

### Cash reporting and payroll are dated tasks

The weekly cash assignment has a **Download bank extract at request date** button. It recreates the same bank information that was available when the request arrived, even when you complete the response later. Do not replace that figure with a later month-end balance. The written forecast/collection assumptions still need professional review.

The payroll review arrives before payday. It asks you to use the authorised final register, reconcile net wages and investigate a specifically rejected overtime amount. Reviewing that register is separate from posting the payroll on its appropriate date and subsequently reconciling its liability settlements. The app does not execute the payment.

## The inbox reacts to your work

Requests become available according to the PC date. Source deliveries are grouped into messages rather than producing one notification for every invoice. Bank-feed messages link to the available extract. Read/unread status is saved.

Responses appear as fictional sent/draft correspondence. Submitted responses and blockers can receive a next-working-day acknowledgement. The bank-detail-change exercise has a defined branch: an unsafe instruction fails the control check; an independently verified hold gets a simulated callback outcome. Other management replies are primarily acknowledgements and follow-up instructions, not unrestricted conversational AI or independent approval of your prose.

Due-date reminders and overdue escalations keep missed commitments visible. A jump from the 1st to the 14th releases the intervening messages and work; it does not mark them completed. Historical missed-deadline correspondence remains after late completion. The date log records the jump, note and outstanding/crossed task IDs. The sent/draft representation retains the latest response, not an immutable version of every email you ever drafted.

No live email provider is connected. No message is sent to a real person, bank or tax authority.

## Waiting is not the same as done

Record a blocker, named owner and follow-up date when an input is missing. The assignment becomes **Waiting**, remains open and retains its deadline. Acknowledgement does not waive the task or create new evidence.

The prior-year AFS request deliberately records an evidence gap. A simulated board-secretary response does not manufacture signed statements or full comparatives that are absent from the case. Your response should identify what is needed, who must provide it and what conclusion remains unsigned.

A completed control task does not establish that a real independent bank approver has approved anything. Recommendations, authorisation and actual execution remain distinct.

## Checks, feedback and close

**Check today's work** assesses available source/date tasks, correct-source bank matches, daily responses and released formal deliverables. It excludes future activity. Open a task to see its saved-work status and, when checked/submitted, relevant numerical differences. Late work, missing work, incorrect work, drafts and blockers have distinct states.

**Full month review** opens only with the closing evidence. It retains the existing independent accounting checks, balance/report feedback, extra/misdated posting findings and attempt history. Daily management requests are now a separate assessment area. Incomplete daily responses also prevent a new daily-mode month close. Changed daily responses make the previous monthly review stale.

The daily queue does not replace a complete ledger assessment: additional ad hoc journals outside the defined daily source/date tasks require the full month review. A matching debit and credit does not establish correct accounts.

Written judgement, the realism of cash-flow assumptions, a freeform workbook's cell-by-cell reasoning and complete statutory AFS disclosure are **not independently graded**. Submit the requested numerical reconciliation/report outputs through the formal deliverable, and treat qualitative content as a portfolio for separate review. Minimum response length is an input-completeness rule, not a quality score.

Export today's check and the day log as CSV. Full backups contain the saved responses, completion dates, clock, history, journals, bank matches, formal work and saved desktop files.

## Save safely

The date-change flow refuses to proceed while response, spreadsheet or local accounting-form drafts are unsaved. Accounting-form drafts survive navigating away or closing their window; reopen the accounting window to finish them, or use **Discard accounting drafts** explicitly. Closing an accounting window does not post or discard its draft automatically.

Daily-response close buttons offer Save draft & close, Discard response or Cancel. A failed or uncertain save keeps the response open. Reload after a lost acknowledgement before retrying. When the saved case or response has changed, download the preserved old draft and explicitly load the current saved version instead of overwriting it blindly. Invalid numerical input remains visible for correction.

Original evidence is read-only. Spreadsheet snapshots are not live data feeds and do not automatically update after later postings. The existing Excel-like editing, formulas, tabs, formatting, copy/paste, XLSX export/import and close controls remain in the workstation. This is not Microsoft Excel; prior workbook limits still apply.

Local storage depends on the browser and file origin. The taskbar explicitly shows **Preview memory only** when persistence is unavailable. Export backups before closing in that mode and regularly in every mode. Downloaded backup data, not an unsaved screen, is your portable record.

## Scope of realism

This is a fictional **single-entity AUD, calendar-2025 teaching case**, aimed at practising the preparation and ownership responsibilities that differ from audit. The CA(SA) background informs the task depth; it does not turn the Australian teaching assumptions into South African legislation.

The case is deterministic, with recurring patterns and a limited number of scripted response branches. It does not cover every industry, multiple legal entities, consolidation, operational multicurrency, variable transaction volumes, full statutory comparative AFS, all awards/payroll obligations, bank payment execution or external approval workflows. There are no legal deadline claims or live tax/payroll rates in this continuation. Use the supplied dated assumptions and retain uncertainty where evidence is missing.

The simulation can provide substantial structured practice and an evidence-backed work portfolio. Completing it is not certification that every real-world finance role or professional judgement has been mastered.

## Implementation and continuation map

`lib/accounting/workday-calendar.ts` defines dates, delivery rules and the projected available company. `workday-types.ts` defines optional saved daily state. `workday.ts` creates deterministic tasks/mail, grades defined task outputs, records blockers/clock changes and enforces boundaries. `engine.ts` validates and persists every mutation through the existing command architecture. `month-assessment.ts` integrates daily requests and stale-review fingerprints.

`lib/desktop/day-desk.ts` implements Today, assignment responses, checks and the end-day calendar. `shell.ts` handles navigation, the clock, mail and window lifecycles. `files.ts` supplies dated sources/workbooks. The standalone uses the actual accounting engine through `scripts/desktop/local-accounting.ts` and `preview.ts`. React integration changes are included in the source but require the full dependency-backed application checks before deployment.

The full independent scenario remains internal to marking; normal UI/export consumers receive the date-projected company. This is a self-study tool, not a proctored security boundary: source code and test fixtures can reveal solutions. Do not embed generated solved test fixtures in the distributable HTML or source inventory.

See `DAY-BY-DAY-V3-VERIFICATION.md` for executed commands, results and unverified integration boundaries.
